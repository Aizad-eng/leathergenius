What we make things from, and how we check them

The leathers, trims and labels we work with in Sialkot, what your branding adds to an order, and what we look at before a run ships. If you need something that isn’t listed here, ask us.

On this page: Materials, linings and hardware, branding, quality control, the workshop, who we work with.

Leathers and cloth

We buy our hides ourselves. Each style is tied to one named leather, weight and finish, and the whole run is cut from that, so the last jacket matches the sample you approved.

Leathers and cloth we cut from, with weights and typical uses
MaterialWeight or finishWhat we use it for

We run both veg-tan and chrome-tan lines, and we can hand-stitch a piece all the way through.

What we check before a run ships

We inspect every run before it leaves Sialkot. We check the hide grade against the leather agreed for your style, measure stitch density, cycle the zips and hardware, and go over the lining and the labels. If we flag a unit, it gets remade, and the shipment waits until it is.

You’re welcome to send your own inspector before a run ships, or to come and see the floor.

Who we work with
