Quality control

Every run is inspected before it leaves Sialkot.

Pre-shipment inspection is part of every order, not an add-on. If a unit fails a check, the shipment waits until that unit is remade.

Pre-shipment checklist

  • Hide grade verification. The leather is checked against the named hide, weight and finish locked at sample.
  • Stitch density. Seams are checked against the stitch spec of your approved sample.
  • Zipper and hardware cycling. Zips, snaps and buckles are worked through before packing.
  • Lining and label check. Linings, neck labels and tags are checked against your spec.
Shipment holds. Flagged units are remade before anything ships. You do not receive the rejects.
Your eyes on the floor. Send your own inspector, or visit the floor yourself. You are welcome either way.
