The four steps

From first message to your dock.

About three weeks from first message to an approved sample and your quote. Then 4 to 6 weeks of production before your goods ship.

Quality

Nothing ships until it passes inspection.

Every run includes a pre-shipment inspection. If a unit is flagged, the shipment holds until it is remade. You are welcome to send your own inspector or visit the floor.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask before the first order.

Not covered here? Write to queries@leathergenius.site. We reply within 24 hours on weekdays.

What is your minimum order?

50 to 100 units per style for production. Jackets usually start at 100, bags and structured pieces from 50. Samples have no minimum.

How is pricing worked out?

After you approve the sample, we quote a per-unit landed cost, tiered at 100, 250, 500, 1,000 and 2,500 units. Production usually comes in 30 to 40% below US domestic make from 250 units up, and 15 to 25% below under that. We do not publish a price list, because the number depends on your hide, lining, hardware and volume.

How do you check quality?

Every run gets a pre-shipment inspection: hide grade verification, stitch density, zipper and hardware cycling, and a lining and label check. The shipment holds until any flagged units are remade.

Can you put our brand on it?

Yes. Debossed, hot-foiled, laser-etched or stitched marks, plus woven neck labels, hang tags, dust bags and boxes to your spec. Branding adds 5 to 10 days and a small per-unit cost.

What are the payment terms?

Nothing until you approve the sample. Then 50% deposit and 50% on shipment, by wire or international ACH. Repeat orders can move to net 30 after the first successful run.

Can I send you a piece to copy?

Yes. Mail us a piece you already sell, or describe the style, and we match it. We then lock the hide, weight, lining, hardware, stitch and finish with you before we build the sample.

Is the sample really free?

Yes. We build the first piece free and courier it to you free by FedEx. There is no sample fee, no minimum on samples and no commitment to order.

How long do the sample and production take?

Sample turnaround is 7 to 10 days. Production takes 4 to 6 weeks after you place the order. Branding adds 5 to 10 days.

Do you handle shipping and customs?

Yes. Finished orders go by air or sea depending on how urgently you need them. Customs is handled, and the goods land at your dock or warehouse.

Can I visit or send an inspector?

Yes. Buyers are welcome to send an inspector or visit the floor in Sialkot.

What can you make besides jackets?

Bags, small leather goods and waxed-twill outerwear, all to your spec. That covers weekenders, briefcases and totes, belts, and twill or canvas chore coats and field jackets, in lambskin, cowhide and steer, goat and suede, or twill and canvas.
