How an order works, and what it costs

The steps from your first email to the goods arriving at your door, our terms, how we price, and the questions people ask us most.

On this page: The steps, terms, pricing, inspection, questions.

The steps

  1. You post us a piece you already sell, or describe the style by email. If you’re posting it, write first and we’ll send you the address.
  2. Before we cut anything, we agree the spec with you: the hide, its weight and finish, the lining, the zips and hardware, the stitching, and any branding. That spec stays tied to the style from then on.
  3. We make the sample in Sialkot and send it by FedEx at our cost. It usually takes 7 to 10 days. There’s no fee and no minimum, and you aren’t committing to an order.
  4. If you approve the sample, we send a per-unit landed-cost quote at 100, 250, 500, 1,000 and 2,500 units. You choose the quantity and pay a 50% deposit.
  5. Production takes 4 to 6 weeks. If the piece carries your branding, allow another 5 to 10 days.
  6. Before anything ships, we inspect the run. Anything we flag is remade first. You can send your own inspector at this point, or visit.
  7. We ship by air or sea, depending on how soon you need the goods, and you pay the other 50% by wire or international ACH. We handle customs, and the order arrives at your dock or warehouse. Once a first run has gone well, repeat orders can move to net 30.

How we price

We don’t keep a price list. What a piece costs depends on the hide, the lining and hardware, your branding, and how many you order, so we quote once you’ve approved the sample and we know exactly what we’re making.

The quote gives you one landed price per unit at each of five quantities. That way you can see how the price moves before you decide how many to order.

Inspection

Every run is inspected before it ships. We check the hide grade, stitch density, the zips and hardware, and the lining and labels. If a unit doesn’t pass, we remake it and the shipment waits for it. You’re welcome to send your own inspector, or to come to Sialkot and see the floor.

Questions

Is the sample really free?
Yes. We build the first piece and send it by FedEx at our cost. There’s no fee, no minimum on samples, and you’re not committing to an order.
Can I send you a piece to copy?
Yes. Post it to us, or describe the style by email, and we’ll match it. Write first and we’ll send the address. We agree the hide, weight, lining, hardware, stitching and finish with you before we make the sample.
What’s your minimum order?
50 to 100 units per style. Jackets usually start at 100, bags and structured pieces at 50. Samples have no minimum.
How long does it take?
The sample takes 7 to 10 days. Production takes 4 to 6 weeks after you order, and branding adds 5 to 10 days.
How much will it cost?
We quote after the sample, because the price depends on the hide, the trims, your branding and how many you order. From 250 units it usually comes in 30 to 40% below making the same piece in the US, and 15 to 25% below under that.
What are the payment terms?
Nothing until you approve the sample. Then 50% deposit and 50% on shipment, by wire or international ACH. Repeat orders can move to net 30 after the first successful run.
Can you put our brand on it?
Yes. Debossed, hot-foiled, laser-etched or stitched marks, plus woven neck labels, hang tags, dust bags and boxes. Branding adds 5 to 10 days and a small cost per unit.
How do you check quality?
We inspect every run before it ships: hide grade, stitch density, zips and hardware, lining and labels. If we flag a unit, it’s remade before the shipment leaves.
Do you handle shipping and customs?
Yes. We send finished orders by air or sea, depending on how soon you need them. We handle customs, and the goods arrive at your dock or warehouse.
Can we visit, or send an inspector?
Yes. You can come to Sialkot, or send an inspector to check a run before it ships.
Do you make anything besides jackets?
Yes. Bags, small leather goods and twill outerwear, all to your spec. The catalogue only shows jackets so far, so for anything else, send us the piece or describe it. Bag runs start at 50 units.
Who do you usually work with?
Independent brands. Outerwear and moto labels, bag and travel brands, and boutiques adding their own leather line. We’re not set up for the big chains.

Anything else, write to queries@leathergenius.site. We reply within 24 hours on weekdays.
