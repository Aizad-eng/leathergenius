Questions buyers ask

Before you send a sample request.

Something else on your mind? Write to queries@leathergenius.site. We reply within 24 hours on weekdays.

What is your minimum order?

50 to 100 units per style for production. Jackets usually start at 100, bags and structured pieces from 50. Samples have no minimum.

How is pricing worked out?

After you approve the sample, we quote a per-unit landed cost, tiered at 100, 250, 500, 1,000 and 2,500 units. Production usually comes in 30 to 40% below US domestic make from 250 units up, and 15 to 25% below under that.

How do you check quality?

Every run gets a pre-shipment inspection: hide grade, stitch density, zipper and hardware cycling, lining and label check. The shipment holds until any flagged units are remade. You are welcome to send an inspector or visit the floor.

Can you put our brand on it?

Yes. Debossed, hot-foiled, laser-etched or stitched marks, plus woven neck labels, hang tags, dust bags and boxes to your spec. Branding adds 5 to 10 days and a small per-unit cost.

What are the payment terms?

Nothing until you approve the sample. Then 50% deposit and 50% on shipment, by wire or international ACH. Repeat orders can move to net 30 after the first successful run.
