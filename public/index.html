A 30-year export workshop, without the middleman.

Leather Genius is how smaller brands reach a workshop that’s been exporting for three decades. We’ve made leather for export for more than thirty years, and a good number of US jacket and tack brands already sell pieces from our floors under their own labels.

We started Leather Genius so smaller brands could reach us without a middleman. You write to the people who will make your jackets. You hear back within a day, and there’s no broker’s margin in the price.

We buy our hides ourselves. Each style is tied to a named leather, weight and finish, so the hundredth jacket matches the sample you signed off. Runs start at 50 units for bags and around 100 for jackets, and we’ve kept the same standard on orders of 10,000.

Most of the people we make for run independent outerwear and moto labels, bag and travel brands, or boutiques starting a leather line of their own. If that’s you, send us a style and we’ll take it from there.

Questions

Do you make bags, or only jackets?
Both. The catalogue only shows jackets so far, but we make bags and small leather goods too. Bag runs start at 50 units.
Can you copy a piece we already sell?
Yes. Post it to us and we’ll match the cut, hide, hardware and finish for the sample. Email us first and we’ll send the address.
How much will it cost?
We quote after the sample, because the price depends on the hide, the trims and how many you order. From 250 units it usually comes in 30 to 40% below making the same piece in the US.
Who do you usually work with?
Independent brands. Moto and outerwear labels, bag makers, boutiques adding their own line. We’re not set up for the big chains.
Can we visit?
Yes. You can visit the workshop, or send an inspector to check a run before it leaves.

Anything else, write to stella@getleathergenius.com. We reply within 24 hours on weekdays.
