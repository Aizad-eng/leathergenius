Custom styles

Don't see your style? Send it to us.

This page is a starting point, not the limit. Mail us a piece you already sell, or send a tech pack or a few photos. We match it, build the first sample free and courier it to you by DHL Express.

No sample fee, no MOQ on samples, no commitment. Sample turnaround is 7 to 10 days. Production MOQ is 50 to 100 units per style.

Send us your style →

Every style can be built in

  • Lambskin, 0.7 to 0.9 mm, for bombers, blazers and shirt-jackets.
  • Cowhide and steer, 1.1 to 1.4 mm, for bikers and structured pieces.
  • Goat and suede, pebble or nap, for truckers and overshirts.
  • Twill and canvas, 10 to 14 oz, for chore coats and field jackets.
  • Linings: Bemberg or quilted.
  • Hardware: YKK or RIRI, in solid brass or antique nickel.
  • Your label: debossed, hot-foiled, laser-etched or stitched marks, woven neck labels, hang tags, dust bags and boxes to spec.
