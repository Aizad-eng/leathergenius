What happens next

  1. We reply by email within 24 hours on weekdays, with any questions about the style.
  2. We agree the hide, weight, lining, hardware, stitching and finish with you before we cut anything.
  3. We make the sample and send it by FedEx. That takes 7 to 10 days.
  4. If you approve it, we send a per-unit landed-cost quote. If you don’t, you owe us nothing.
